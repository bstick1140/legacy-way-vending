# Legacy Way Vending — Design Brainstorm

## Context
Target audience: Middle and high school principals in Upstate South Carolina.
Goal: Capture attention, build trust, drive qualification form completions.
Brand: Certified Free Healthy Vending partner. Local, professional, health-forward.

---

<response>
<idea>
**Design Movement:** Bold Organic Modernism — where clean, editorial structure meets earthy vitality

**Core Principles:**
1. Confident asymmetry: offset text blocks against full-bleed imagery to create tension and visual interest
2. Organic warmth: earthy greens and amber tones that feel fresh and natural, not clinical
3. Typographic authority: large-scale display type that commands attention like a school hallway banner
4. Conversion-first layout: every scroll reveals a new reason to act, culminating in the qualification form

**Color Philosophy:**
- Deep forest green (#1B4332) as the anchor — conveys health, trust, and growth
- Warm amber/gold (#F59E0B) as the action color — energy, optimism, calls-to-action
- Off-white cream (#FAFAF7) for backgrounds — warmth without sterility
- Charcoal (#1C1C1E) for body text — readable, premium
- Emotional intent: principals should feel they are choosing something that elevates their school's culture

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Diagonal section dividers (clip-path) to break the monotony of horizontal bands
- Hero: full-bleed split layout — left 60% bold typography, right 40% machine imagery with a green overlay
- Features: staggered card grid with alternating text/image columns
- Qualification form: centered spotlight section with a dark green background and glowing amber CTA

**Signature Elements:**
1. Leaf/organic line motifs as decorative dividers and section accents
2. Large numerals (01, 02, 03) as section anchors — editorial magazine style
3. Pill-shaped badges ("FREE", "USDA Approved", "No Cost") in amber on green

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Scroll-triggered entrance animations (fade-up) for each section
- Hover states on cards reveal a green overlay with a brief description
- Form steps animate with a sliding progress bar in amber

**Animation:**
- Entrance: elements fade-up with 0.3s ease-out, staggered 0.1s between children
- Progress bar in qualification form fills with a smooth amber gradient
- CTA buttons: subtle scale(1.02) on hover with a shadow bloom

**Typography System:**
- Display: "Playfair Display" — bold, editorial, authoritative (headlines)
- Body: "DM Sans" — clean, modern, approachable (paragraphs, labels)
- Accent: "DM Mono" — for stats, numbers, and badges
</idea>

## Selected Design
**Approach 1: Bold Organic Modernism** — Deep forest green + warm amber, editorial typography with Playfair Display + DM Sans, diagonal section dividers, and a conversion-first layout with scroll-triggered animations.
